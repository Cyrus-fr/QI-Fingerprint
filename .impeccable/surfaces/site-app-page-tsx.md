# Surface brief — QI-Fingerprint site (`site/`, single scrolling route + per-seed routes)

**Visitor mode:** Experience. The artifact leads from the first frame; the interface recedes.

**Audience & job:** Security researchers and applied cryptographers, arriving skeptical,
deciding in about ninety seconds whether this is real work. They skim for rigour, pick the
one section that would expose shallow work, and read that properly.

**Task / action:** Descend the evidence and come away convinced. There is no signup, no
purchase, no download. The action is conviction, and the repro command is how they test it.

**Proof / content (nothing may be dropped):** every figure, table and repro command; the
stated limits; both data figures (cohort propagation graph, cascade fan); the real mainnet
control at block 252,474; the current project state. Content is fixed — only presentation changes.

**Brief-pinned constraints (these beat the roll):** flashy, clean, cinematic, animation-rich,
at or above Awwwards portfolio craft. Resolved against the user's other stated wrong —
"decoration that isn't data" — by making the motion *be* the measurement: nothing animates
that is not a reading arriving.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The evidence read as a cored section — one continuous depth axis the visitor
descends, where every hatch, width and stroke is a measurement, and the descent halts at the
single real key recovered from block 252,474. It refuses this category's default arrangement:
a centred claim over a terminal transcript, followed by a row of feature cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Wet slate `#232a27` ground (split core under flat light, deliberately not
black); pale log stock `#dde2d6` for reading columns; dry rock `#8e968a` secondary ink; field
ink `#1d5c8a` for annotation and marked intervals; assay `#a8442f` used exactly once on the
page, on the one recovered key. Archivo carries all type, Spline Sans Mono every measured
value. One committed ink weight throughout. Lithology hatch patterns carry class identity, so
nothing reads by hue alone. A survey title block stands in for a hero. No card shells, no
gradients, no glass, no glow.

**STORY:** The visitor understands that the unit of analysis is the population rather than the
key; believes it because every reading carries its check and its repro command; and descends
far enough to meet the one real recovered key and the honest limits beneath it.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** No headline. Full-bleed section: the depth column holds the left third,
running block 250,000 at the top to 252,474 at the fold. Three live tracks read beside it —
signature density, bias magnitude, collision markers — drawing themselves as the scan
descends. The assay mark sits at its true depth, annotated in field ink with txid and derived
address. A survey title block anchors bottom-left carrying sheet totals: 1,026 blocks,
719,121 signatures, 1 hit. The primary action is the descent itself; the depth cursor is the
only control.

**FORM:** The Core Log — geological core logs and downhole well surveys. Candidate 4 of 7 on
the resonance-ordered grounded list. Seed key `1e1a1bff`, assigned index 4, mode experience.

Raises, each from a declined challenger, all six binding:
- *the row is the ruler* (VU-meter bridge) — the whole population reads in one sweep, no drill-down required.
- *one surface, one pass* (floated pigment) — a single continuous section, never a stack of panels.
- *one confident ink weight* (rubber-hose cel) — one committed line weight across the drawing system.
- *the figure is the label* (Fletcher poster) — every interval leads with its drawing; text confirms.
- *labelled beyond colour* (colour-chord canopy) — every class carries a hatch, and cohort overlap is itself a readable state.
- *the long axis is the navigation* (indoor weather sun) — you move by descending; there is no other primary nav.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
